# Teaching & Outreach

I help students and practitioners connect molecular methods with questions in forest health and ecology.

## Teaching and mentoring

My teaching experience at the University of Georgia includes guest lectures in forest health and protection, silviculture, tree resistance mechanisms, and community forestry and arboriculture. I have also guest lectured in Forest Health Management at Mississippi State University.

I mentor undergraduate thesis researchers and provide technical and analytical guidance to graduate students and visiting scientists. Topics include experimental design, fungal biology, systematics, phylogenetics, and molecular approaches to trophic ecology.

## Resources for landowners and forest managers

My outreach contributions include resources on brown spot needle blight, needlecast diseases, pine pitch canker, and oak wilt, as well as modules for the University of Florida Forest Landowner Academy. See the [Publications page]({{ '/publications.html' | relative_url }}) for extension publications and technical reports.

## Professional service

I serve as Vice Chair of the American Phytopathological Society’s Forest Pathology Committee (since August 2026) and participate in the Pine Pandemic Preparedness Plan’s Needle Disease Working Group. My service also includes peer review for journals in fungal biology, forest pathology, and forest ecology.
