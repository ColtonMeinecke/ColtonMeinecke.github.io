## From the woods to the command line

My background spans disease characterization and management in agriculture, public health, and forest systems. Now grounded in improving our understanding of tree pathogen biology and developing sound management strategies, my research draws on this experience to explore the complexities of forest disease.

Alongside my research, I mentor students, teach forest health and molecular methods, and develop resources for landowners and forestry professionals.
