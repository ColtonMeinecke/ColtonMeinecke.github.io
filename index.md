## From the laboratory to the forest

My background spans forest health diagnostics with the Wisconsin Department of Natural Resources, public health microbiology with the West Virginia Office of Laboratory Services, and research and laboratory management at UGA. I bring that experience to collaborative projects addressing forest health challenges.

Alongside my research, I mentor students, teach forest health and molecular methods, and develop resources for landowners and forestry professionals.
